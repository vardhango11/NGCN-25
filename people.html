Next Gen Computing and Networking

NGCN is a research group led by Prof. Desikan, focusing on cutting-edge advancements in computing and networking technologies. Our team of over 100 student researchers is dedicated to exploring and developing innovative solutions for the next generation of computing and communication systems.
