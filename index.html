About the NGCN Research Group

The Next Gen Computing and Networking (NGCN) research group is a dynamic and interdisciplinary team of researchers dedicated to exploring the frontiers of computing and networking. Our mission is to develop innovative solutions that address the evolving challenges of modern networks and computing systems. We focus on both theoretical foundations and practical applications, fostering a collaborative environment that encourages creativity and excellence.
